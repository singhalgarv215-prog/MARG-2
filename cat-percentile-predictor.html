How CAT Percentile is Calculated

CAT percentile is not your raw score — it's your relative performance compared to all test takers. A 99 percentile means you scored better than 99% of all candidates who appeared for CAT that year.

The official CAT percentile is calculated using a normalization process across different slots and sessions. This predictor uses historical score-to-percentile data from CAT 2019-2024 to give you an accurate estimate based on your mock performance.

Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is this CAT percentile predictor?
This predictor is based on historical CAT score-to-percentile data from 2019-2024. It gives you a reliable estimate within ±2 percentile points for most score ranges. Actual CAT percentile depends on the difficulty of your specific slot and the performance of all candidates in that year.
Can I use mock test scores for this predictor?
Yes — but note that mock tests from different institutes have varying difficulty levels. SimCAT (IMS) and AIMCAT (CL) are closest to actual CAT difficulty. If you're using easier mocks your actual CAT percentile might be lower than predicted.
What percentile do I need for IIM ABC?
For IIM Ahmedabad, Bangalore, and Calcutta you typically need 99+ percentile in the general category. Each IIM also has sectional cutoffs — usually 85-90 percentile in each section individually. Academics and work experience also play a role in the shortlisting process.
How is VARC scored in CAT 2026?
VARC has 24 questions with a maximum of 72 marks (3 marks per correct answer, -1 for wrong MCQ answers). TITA questions have no negative marking. The section covers Reading Comprehension (16 questions) and Verbal Ability (8 questions).
Is 6 months enough to improve my CAT percentile?
Yes — 6 months of structured preparation is sufficient to significantly improve your CAT percentile. The key is identifying your specific weak areas early and targeting them systematically rather than studying all sections equally.
