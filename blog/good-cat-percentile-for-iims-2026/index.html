What Is a Good CAT Percentile for IIMs in 2026?

Marg Team · 27 September 2026 · 5 min read

In this article
  1. What percentile do the top IIMs need?
  2. What percentile do newer IIMs need?
  3. What percentile is needed by category?
  4. Why percentile does not guarantee a call
  5. What percentile should you target?
  6. FAQs

Quick answer: For General category candidates, 99+ percentile is needed for the older, top-tier IIMs (Ahmedabad, Bangalore, Calcutta), 97-99 percentile for IIMs like Lucknow, Kozhikode and Indore, and 85-95 percentile can be enough for newer or "baby" IIMs (Cracku, IMS India). Category-relaxed candidates (SC/ST/EWS/PwD) generally need a lower percentile, sometimes as low as 40-60 depending on the IIM and category (Collegedunia).

That single number, though, hides more than it reveals - so here's the full picture broken down properly.

What percentile do the top IIMs actually need?

For IIM Ahmedabad, Bangalore, and Calcutta, General-category candidates should target 99+ percentile - and it's worth noting IIM Ahmedabad recently raised its own qualifying cutoff from 80 to 95 percentile, the sharpest jump of any IIM in recent years (IMS India). For IIM Bangalore specifically, anything below 99 percentile makes a General-category call difficult unless the candidate has an exceptional academic record, strong work experience, or diversity weightage (IMS India).

Across all IIMs combined, the overall cutoff for General/NC-OBC candidates typically lands in the 97-100 percentile range for the older, more competitive campuses (Career Launcher).

What percentile do newer / "baby" IIMs need?

Newer IIMs are considerably more accessible. Candidates in the 85-99+ percentile range are realistic contenders at baby IIMs, and even a percentile between 90 and 95 can be enough for a shortlist at several campuses depending on the rest of the candidate's profile (Cracku, GuideMeEducation). IIM Bangalore's own first-shortlist criteria in a recent cycle listed a minimum overall percentile of just 85 for General candidates before sectional and profile factors were applied (Cracku).

What percentile is needed by category?

  • General: 97-100 percentile for top IIMs; 85-95 for newer IIMs (Shiksha)
  • OBC/NC-OBC: Slightly relaxed versus General, campus-dependent
  • SC/ST/PwD: Can range as low as 40-60 percentile at several IIMs, depending on the campus and category weightage applied (Collegedunia)
  • EWS: Falls between General and reserved-category cutoffs, again campus-specific

Because these ranges shift by admission cycle, always cross-check the exact figure against the current year's official IIM admission criteria before finalising a target.

Why a "good enough" percentile still doesn't guarantee a call

This is the part most aspirants misunderstand: IIM admissions are not decided by percentile alone. Most IIMs build a composite score using your CAT percentile alongside academics, work experience, gender diversity, and other profile factors - which is why two candidates with the identical percentile can get completely different outcomes (Career Launcher, GoalISB).

The gap can cut both ways. A candidate with three years of strong work experience and solid academics might get a call at 98.5 percentile, while a fresher at 99.5 percentile with an average academic record gets rejected at the very same IIM (GoalISB). Meeting the published "qualifying cutoff" is not the same as the real, effective percentile most successful candidates actually convert at - that number is usually meaningfully higher (GuideMeEducation).

The practical takeaway: don't prepare toward the minimum published cutoff. Build in a safety margin, because the "real" cutoff for your specific profile is almost always higher than the official minimum.

So what percentile should you actually target?

If your goal is a top-6 IIM, treat 99+ as your baseline target, not your ceiling - aim meaningfully above it if your academic and work-experience profile is average, since a stronger profile can carry a slightly lower percentile but a weaker profile needs the opposite (GoalISB). If you're targeting newer IIMs or strong non-IIM B-schools, a well-prepared 90-95 percentile target with a genuine safety margin is realistic (GuideMeEducation).

FAQs

Is 90 percentile good enough for an IIM? It can be - mainly at newer IIMs and depending on your profile - but it will not be enough for the top-tier, older IIMs, which usually need 97+ (Collegedunia).

Is 95 percentile enough for an IIM call? Possibly, for several IIMs and strong non-IIM B-schools, especially with a solid profile - but it falls short of the 97-99+ range top IIMs typically expect (Shiksha).

Can I get an IIM with a 70 percentile? Only in specific reserved-category cases at certain IIMs; for General category, 70 percentile does not meet even the newer IIMs' realistic range (Collegedunia).

Does a high percentile guarantee an IIM seat? No - IIMs use a composite score combining percentile with academics, work experience, and diversity factors, so percentile alone is necessary but not sufficient (Career Launcher).
