Privacy Policy
Last updated: May 2026

What we collect

When you sign in with Google, we collect your name, email address, and profile photo. To personalise your mentoring across sessions, we may also store your conversation history, study profile, cognitive and behavioural patterns, mock history, practice progress, check-ins, and product-engagement milestones. If you request a community invite, we store the WhatsApp number you submit and the status of that request; the number is saved directly to Supabase and is not sent to Gemini or added to chat history. Friend challenges store the shared question and anonymous events such as link opened, answer submitted, Ask Marg clicked, and completed referral sign-in. The creator’s identity is not shown on the public challenge page.

How we use it

Your data is used solely to provide and improve Marg's mentoring experience.

Data storage

Your data is stored securely on Supabase servers. We do not sell, share, or trade your personal information with any third parties.

Deleting your data

To request deletion of your account and associated data, email support@trymarg.com from the email linked to your Marg account. We will process the request within 7 business days. Clearing a chat or browser storage alone does not delete records stored in Supabase.
