About Marg
Built in 2026

The story

Marg was built by Garv Singhal, a CAT aspirant who gave the exam twice and felt the same frustration both times — not a lack of resources, but a lack of direction. Every study plan he found was generic. Every piece of advice assumed unlimited time. Nobody was telling him specifically what to do given his actual situation.

The gap

Most CAT resources — coaching institutes, YouTube channels, Telegram groups — give the same advice to everyone. But every aspirant's situation is different. Different weak areas, different time constraints, different emotional states. Marg was built to fill that gap.

The name

Marg means "path" in Hindi. The name was chosen deliberately — Marg is not meant to be another resource, it is meant to be your path. Personalised, specific, and built around you.

What's next

Marg is currently focused on CAT 2026 aspirants. Mock analysis, daily check-ins, and study plan exports are coming soon. After CAT, Marg will expand to help the same students with GD-PI preparation and career decisions.
